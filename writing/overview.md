I’m excited to share my project, **Better Resume**. Let’s face it, building a solid resume can be a pain. It often feels overwhelming and tedious. I created this tool to simplify that process, especially for developers and professionals. My goal? To help you create cleaner, more effective resumes in a streamlined and efficient way.

## What's It All About?

The main idea behind Better Resume is to offer a better way to structure and present resumes. When you’re applying for a job, a well-crafted resume can make all the difference. I wanted to create something that took the headache out of resume building. It provides a straightforward workflow and customizable templates that adjust to your needs.

### Core Features

Here’s what you’ll find in the project:

- **Streamlined Workflow**: No more jumping through hoops. My approach is straightforward.
- **Clean, Modern Templates**: Quality matters. The templates are designed to be visually appealing and professional.
- **Customization Options**: Tailor your resume easily to reflect your unique experience and skills.

## The Tech Stack

I built Better Resume with a focus on flexibility and simplicity. The specific stack may vary, but I aimed for something familiar and straightforward:

- **Primary Language**: While I haven't locked it down to one language, it's likely to be JavaScript, Python, or even Markdown. 
- **Templating Engines or Static Site Generators**: I envision utilizing these tools to keep things organized and efficient, making it easy to manage various resume styles.

## Design Decisions

Throughout the development of Better Resume, I made a few key decisions that guided the project:

- **User Experience First**: The primary focus was an intuitive experience. I hate wasting time on tools that aren’t user-friendly, so I made sure to prioritize simplicity.
- **Modular Architecture**: Keeping components modular allows for easier updates and addition of features.
- **Focus on Customization**: I want users to express themselves. A cookie-cutter template doesn’t do that—customization does.

## Trade-offs

Sure, every decision comes with trade-offs. Here are a few I considered:

- **Complexity vs. Usability**: While adding extensive features could make the tool powerful, I opted for an easier path so first-time users wouldn't feel overwhelmed. The goal is to keep it simple but effective.
- **Template Variability**: I want a diverse range of professional templates, but that will take time and effort. Right now, I’m focusing on a couple of solid ones before expanding.

## What’s Next?

I’ve got big plans for Better Resume, and here’s what’s next on my roadmap:
